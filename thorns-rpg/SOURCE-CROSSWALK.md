## Additional source constraints

- WN_TOWER: bio-mod regulation, contract-based access, the Greatweb, WN_EDU, Druid-Sec, Life Strings, Corded Bio-Glass, industrial affiliations and the THORNS. No universal gold economy is inferred.
- CITY_CLOUD_S: metallurgy affiliation, learning under KHO, counter-node risks, upper-sector permissions and OMNI-META.OPEND. Reputation does not bypass security.
- ENACT_HILLS: restricted archives, resident skills, PYRI and autonomy, metal-kin / spark-core, necra-mod certification, digiPRINT and institutional powers. Resurrection remains context-bound.
- AETHERGALE: NanoClad, resource acquisition, arcaneTECH, AETHER, fractURAL affinity, sanctuary, Lila / Khi and an immutable RED>GATE>.
- NULL_FIELD.OMNI: remote observation, query risk, ENCAP.MARKING, unknown structures and adminENACT data.RET.nodes. The source’s unfinished interior account remains unresolved.
- The two maps remain visual references; meters in local interaction rules are not a measured reconstruction of those drawings.

Terms such as fidelity, power stance and meta-break are not assigned unsupported units or universal scalar values. This crosswalk does not claim to quantify every technical noun in the lore.

Remote projections beyond radius 40 draw thematic vocabulary from NULL_FIELD.OMNI but assert no newly verified access. Four extra mods explicitly carry adaptation labels.
